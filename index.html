I am an MSc student in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Amsterdam. I am currently a research intern in the VISTA team at LIX, École Polytechnique, working with Prof. Vicky Kalogeiton. At UvA I completed my MSc thesis with Prof. Efstratios Gavves and Riccardo Valperga.

From June to August 2026 I interned at Damen RD&I on the FastPhysics project. My work there was Geometric Deep Learning for Accelerated High-Fidelity Ship Hull Simulations.

My work sits at the intersection of computer vision, robot learning, and generative models. I am interested in vision-language-action models, deformable-object manipulation, geometric deep learning, and physics-based simulation — methods that help machines understand and act in the physical world. I have a background in Mechanical Engineering from the National Technical University of Athens.

Recently I co-authored InfoCIR, a visual analytics system for composed image retrieval, published at IEEE PacificVis 2026.

Research interests: computer vision · multimodal learning · robot learning · geometric deep learning · generative AI · physical AI

    InfoCIR: Multimedia Analysis for Composed Image Retrieval
    Ioannis Dravilas, Ioannis Kapetangeorgis, Anastasios Latsoudis, Conor McCarthy, Gonçalo Marcelino, and Marcel Worring
    In Proceedings of the 19th IEEE Pacific Visualization Conference (PacificVis), 2026
    Abs DOI arXiv Bib HTML PDF Code

    Composed Image Retrieval (CIR) allows users to search for images by combining a reference image with a text prompt that describes desired modifications. While vision-language models like CLIP have popularized this task by embedding multiple modalities into a joint space, developers still lack tools that reveal how these multimodal prompts interact with embedding spaces and why small wording changes can dramatically alter the results. We present InfoCIR, a visual analytics system that closes this gap by coupling retrieval, explainability, and prompt engineering in a single, interactive dashboard.
