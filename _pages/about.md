I am an MSc student in [Artificial Intelligence](https://www.uva.nl/en/programmes/masters/artificial-intelligence/artificial-intelligence.html) at the [University of Amsterdam](https://www.uva.nl/). I am currently a research intern in the [VISTA](https://www.lix.polytechnique.fr/vista/members/index.html) team at LIX, [École Polytechnique](https://www.polytechnique.edu/), working with [Prof. Vicky Kalogeiton](https://vicky.kalogeiton.info/). At UvA I completed my MSc thesis with [Prof. Efstratios Gavves](https://www.egavves.com/) and [Riccardo Valperga](https://ricvalp.github.io/).

From June to August 2026 I interned at [Damen](https://www.damen.com/) RD&I on the **FastPhysics** project. My work there was *Geometric Deep Learning for Accelerated High-Fidelity Ship Hull Simulations*.

My work sits at the intersection of **computer vision**, **robot learning**, and **generative models**. I am interested in vision-language-action models, deformable-object manipulation, geometric deep learning, and physics-based simulation — methods that help machines understand and act in the physical world. I have a background in Mechanical Engineering from the [National Technical University of Athens](https://www.ntua.gr/en/).

Recently I co-authored [InfoCIR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13402), a visual analytics system for composed image retrieval, published at IEEE PacificVis 2026.

**Research interests:** computer vision · multimodal learning · robot learning · geometric deep learning · generative AI · physical AI
