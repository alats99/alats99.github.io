From June to August 2026 I interned in the RD&I department at [Damen](https://www.damen.com/), on the **FastPhysics** project.

The assignment title was **Geometric Deep Learning for Accelerated High-Fidelity Ship Hull Simulations**. FastPhysics uses geometric deep learning to speed up high-fidelity hull hydrodynamics, so resistance and flow can be estimated from geometry much faster than full CFD.

I contributed as an intern on the Data Science side of the project, working with simulation data and geometry-aware models for ship performance.
