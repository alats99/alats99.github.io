**DeformSim** is a small simulator for testing deformable-object dynamics. I use it as a sandbox for robot-learning experiments on objects that bend, stretch, or fold, where rigid-body assumptions break down.

**Code:** [github.com/alats99/DeformSim](https://github.com/alats99/DeformSim)
