I completed my MSc thesis at the University of Amsterdam with [Prof. Efstratios Gavves](https://www.egavves.com/) and [Riccardo Valperga](https://ricvalp.github.io/).

The thesis sits in robot learning: using vision and generative models so agents can act in the physical world. Related interests include vision-language-action models, deformable-object manipulation, and physics-based simulation.

The manuscript is not publicly available.
